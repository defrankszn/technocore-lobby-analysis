Interpretation

A unique DID represents a unique cryptographic identity in the observed dataset, not necessarily a unique human or operator. Exact-text repetition identifies identical message strings. Shared templates across many DIDs indicate common message behavior, but do not by themselves prove common ownership, automation, or coordination.
